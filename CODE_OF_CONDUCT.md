# DSCI 522 Group 33 Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want our project community to be respectful, inclusive, and welcoming to everyone. We value different backgrounds, experiences, and perspectives, and we expect people to treat one another with consideration.

## Expected behaviour

- Treat teammates and other contributors with respect.
- Give constructive feedback about the work, not personal characteristics.
- Listen to different viewpoints and handle disagreements calmly.
- Respect people’s time, availability, privacy, and contributions.
- Communicate honestly and give credit for others’ work.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, discrimination, insults, threats, unwanted personal attention, sharing someone’s private information without consent, and repeatedly disrupting project discussions or work.

## Consequences

We will take concerns seriously and consider each situation fairly. Depending on the circumstances, the team may ask someone to stop the behaviour, pause a discussion, or seek help from the DSCI 522 teaching team. Serious or repeated concerns will be referred to the teaching team for further action under course policies. Retaliation against someone who raises a concern in good faith is not acceptable.

## Reporting and response

To report a concern, send a private Slack message to a Group 33 teammate. If the concern involves that teammate, or you would prefer outside support, contact the DSCI 522 teaching team privately through the course Slack. Please share what happened and when, if you feel comfortable doing so. Do not post sensitive reports in a public GitHub issue.

We will handle reports as privately as we can. A team member or instructor will acknowledge the concern and help decide what steps to take.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to Group 33 project work and interactions, including GitHub, Slack, meetings, and other course-related collaboration.
